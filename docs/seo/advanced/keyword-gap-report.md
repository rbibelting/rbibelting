## Cannibalization decisions

- Homepage owns broad RBI/Pune supplier intent; division hubs own multi-family discovery; each family URL owns its specific range and selection intent.
- Accessories owns measuring/alignment equipment; V-Belts may link to it without duplicating its full content. Flat Belts owns flat transmission belting; Conveyor Belts owns conveying/material-handling intent.
- Existing search/modal URLs such as `?product=v-belts` canonicalize to the clean division URL and stay outside the sitemap. Product-type fragments remain on their family URL.
- No brand-only pages, city doorway pages, keyword-variant pages, comparison claims, or model-specific pages are added without catalogue evidence and a separate gap review.
- The legacy [rbibelting.in](https://www.rbibelting.in/index.html) site appeared in research. Confirm ownership and whether it should redirect to this site before making any cross-domain migration changes. It is not treated as a competitor.
